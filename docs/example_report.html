Completers vs non-completers

A completer produced observations for every session the dataset expects of that subject; a non-completer produced at least one but not all. The MAR assumption behind the longitudinal estimates is weakest where dropout is caused by the process being studied, so baseline differences between these groups are reported rather than assumed away (§2.5.4). Standardized mean differences are given instead of p-values: the question is whether the groups differ enough to matter, not whether the sample is large enough to detect that they do.

6. Limitations

Secondary families are exploratory, not hypotheses

Only the time × diagnosis interaction is a declared hypothesis. The main effects of time, dx_baseline, and age_baseline are each corrected within their own family and reported for completeness; they were not pre-specified, and reading a small q among them as a finding is exactly the multiplicity error the family declaration exists to prevent.

pybids is not a derivatives parser

pybids indexes raw BIDS well, but its BIDS-Derivatives support is partial, and FreeSurfer's native subject-directory output is not BIDS-organized at all. morphline uses a purpose-built resolver for FreeSurfer conventions and reserves pybids for raw layout traversal inside adapters.

Harmonized results are sensitivity analysis, not inference

Running harmonized and unharmonized models shows how conclusions depend on the harmonization assumption. It does not establish which is correct. Where scanner is confounded with time, the biological longitudinal effect and the scanner effect are not identifiable from the observed data alone — a property of the study design, not a deficiency in the software.

Missingness is assumed missing-at-random

Mixed-effects models use all available observations and handle unbalanced longitudinal designs naturally, but validity rests on a MAR assumption. That assumption is questionable in aging cohorts, where dropout can be caused by the disease progression under study, and where QC failure is plausibly associated with atrophy severity and motion. v1 does not attempt MNAR modeling — no pattern-mixture models, selection models, or joint dropout modeling.

Synthetic data only

These results come from synthetic fixtures with injected ground truth. That validates statistical recovery — whether the methods recover known truth — and cannot validate that the parser handles real headers and real metadata. Those are different questions, and both are needed. Real-data integration is a later milestone.
